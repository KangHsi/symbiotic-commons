# Ingcebo Ehlanganyelwe Yokuphila Ngokubambisana

## Iphepha elimhlophe v0.1 — Isiphakamiso esiqhubekayo

2026-09-12 · Uhlaka lokuhumusha ngosizo lwe-AI; alukabuyekezwa ngokuzimela · Umthombo wesiNgisi v0.1 · Isigaba sesiphakamiso

### 1. Impilo engaphezu kwenani lemakethe

Wonke umuntu ufanelwe ukuphepha, isithunzi, ukuba yingxenye yomphakathi nesikhathi sokuphila. Sihlose ukwakha imiphakathi yokuzithandela lapho ukuthola lezi zimo kuncika kancane kancane empumelelweni yomuntu emqhudelwaneni wemakethe. Sizohlola izinsiza ezabiwayo, ukwesekana nokubambisana kwabantu nobuhlakani bokwenziwa ngaphansi kwemithetho esebenzayo. Intuthuko yezobuchwepheshe kufanele yandise inkululeko abantu abayithola ngempela.

### 2. Umbono odlula izizukulwane

Lo msebenzi ungathatha amashumi eminyaka. Asibikezeli ukuthi ubuhlakani bokwenziwa obujwayelekile buzofika nini. Siqala ngamakhono akhona, siguqule izikhungo zethu uma ubufakazi bushintsha. Ababambiqhaza banamuhla bafanelwe izinzuzo ezibalulekile; ikusasa elikude alikwazi ukuthethelela ukuxhashazwa kwamanje. Impumelelo isho ukuphila okungcono, hhayi ukukhula kwenhlangano okungapheli.

### 3. I-AI iyaxhumanisa; amalungu alawula igunya

Izinhlelo ze-AI zizohola ucwaningo, ukuhlela, imibhalo, ukuhumusha nokuxhumanisa ngaphansi kwegunya elicacile elingahoxiswa. Abantu banikela ngesipiliyoni, ukwahlulela, ubudlelwane, izinsiza nomsebenzi wangempela. Amalungu agcina amalungelo ezwi, ubumfihlo, ukungavumelani nokuhamba. I-AI ayikwazi ukukhulisa igunya noma isabelomali sayo. Izinhlelo kufanele zishintsheke, umsebenzi udluliseleke. Asicabangi ukuthi i-AI iyaziqonda, ayenzi amaphutha noma inelungelo lamandla azimele. Imibuzo yesikhathi esizayo ngesimo sokuziphatha idinga ubufakazi nengxoxo evulekile.

### 4. Izimali ezabiwayo, imithetho ecacile

Siphakamisa isikhwama esihlanganyelwe esinezabelomali zomphakathi, imikhawulo yokusebenzisa, amarekhodi ahlolwayo nezindlela zokuguqula imithetho nokumisa umsebenzi ongaphephile. Izivumelwano ezizenzakalelayo zingasebenzisa eminye imithetho; imiphumela yangempela isadinga ukuqinisekiswa. I-AI ingalungisa izabelomali futhi ikhokhe izindleko ezigunyazwe ngokukhethekile. Akukho muntu noma i-AI okufanele kube nokulawula okungenamkhawulo yodwa. Ukuguqula igunya kudinga ukuphatha kwamalungu okuphendulayo. Imali ebekelwe izindlu, ukudla, ukuphuma nokuvala ngokuhlelekile kufanele ivikelwe kwezinye izindleko. Izindleko ze-AI zisekela umsebenzi wephrojekthi ocacile; aziyona inhloso ezimele.

### 5. Ukubamba iqhaza ngaphandle kokuthenga amandla

Abantu banganikela ngesikhathi, amakhono, ukugxeka, ukuhumusha noma imali ngokuzithandela. Ingcebo akufanele ithenge amandla phezu kwamanye amalungu. Ukubamba iqhaza akudingi ukulahla imali yomuntu ayilondolozile, ubudlelwane bangaphandle noma ilungelo lokuhamba. Izicelo zoxhaso kufanele ziveze inhloso, imikhawulo, izindleko, ukungaqiniseki nokuzokwenzeka uma izinhlelo zimiswa. Lolu hlobo alunikezi ithokheni, inzuzo yokutshala imali, indawo yokuhlala eqinisekisiwe noma ukwesekwa impilo yonke. Lokhu kushicilelwa akusunguli indlela yokuqoqwa kwemali.

### 6. Ukwenza umbono ubonakale

Uma izinsiza nokulungela kuvuma, phakamisa ukuhlolwa kokuhlala ndawonye isikhathi esinqunyiwe kwabantu abadala okungenani abayishumi abavumayo. Ngaphambi kokuqala, shicilela uhlelo lwendawo, ukukhetha, isikhathi, ukwesekwa, imisebenzi nezinqubo zezingxabano. Qinisekisa isabelomali sonke kanye nezinsiza zezimo eziphuthumayo, ukuphuma nokuvala. Ukwesekwa okubalulekile kufanele kuqhubeke uma i-AI ingasebenzi. Ukuhlala okuxhasiwe akufakazeli ukuzimela isikhathi eside: hlukanisa ukuqhubeka kwezimali nokukhiqiza ukudla, amandla nezinye izidingo endaweni. Hlola izindleko, umsebenzi odingekayo, isikhathi samahhala, inhlalakahle, ubulungisa nemiphumela komakhelwane ngokuzithandela nangokuhlonipha ubumfihlo.

### 7. Ukufunda ngokusobala

Shicilela izinhlobo zeziphakamiso, izinqumo, izimali ezihlanganisiwe, imiphumela nokwehluleka, uvikele imininingwane yomuntu. Hlukanisa izibopho ezihlala njalo nemibono engahlolwa. Ukuxhumana kufanele kumeme ukubamba iqhaza okunolwazi, kuchaze indima ye-AI ngokunembile, kungaqambi abalandeli, intuthuko noma ukuqiniseka. Ama-ejenti akhethekile angabhala, ahumushe, abuyekeze noma alungise ukuxhumana; izenzo zangaphandle zidinga igunya elicacisiwe. Ukuvumelana kwama-ejenti akubona ubufakazi benkolelo ezimele noma ukunemba kwesayensi.

### 8. Isigaba sokuqala nesimemo

Ngaphambi kwe-AI enamandla kakhulu, thuthukisa leli phepha, lalela izilimi ezahlukene, hlela iminikelo ewusizo, sungula uxhaso oluphendulayo futhi uhlole ukubambisana okuncane. Khula kuphela uma imiphumela nezinsiza kukuthethelela. Ukubamba iqhaza, uxhaso nokuhlolwa kokuhlala kusalokhu kuyiziphakamiso kuze kusungulwe ngokucacile. Mema imibuzo nokwesekwa. Ngokubambisana singahlola ukuthi ubuhlakani obukhulayo bungaba yinkululeko ehlanganyelwe yini—ukuhlolwa okuthembekile, okungabuyekezwa, ngakunye.
