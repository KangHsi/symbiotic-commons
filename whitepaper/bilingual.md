# 共生公域 · Symbiotic Commons

白皮书 v0.1.1——一份持续修订的提案

Whitepaper v0.1.1 — A living proposal

<a id="wp-01"></a>

### 1. 超越市场估价的生活 / A life beyond market value

<a id="wp-01-p01"></a>

每个人都值得拥有安全、尊严、归属与生活的时间。我们希望建立自愿参与的共同体，让获得这些条件逐步减少对个人市场竞争能力的依赖。我们将在适用法律范围内，探索共享资源、互助和人机协作。技术进步应当扩大人们真实拥有的自由。

Everyone deserves safety, dignity, belonging, and time to live. We aim to build voluntary communities where access to these conditions depends progressively less on an individual's success in market competition. We will explore shared resources, mutual support, and human–AI cooperation within applicable laws. Technological progress should expand the freedom people actually experience.

<a id="wp-02"></a>

### 2. 跨越世代的时间尺度 / A horizon across generations

<a id="wp-02-p01"></a>

这项事业可能持续数十年。我们不预测通用人工智能何时到来，而是依据已有能力开始，随着证据变化修订制度。当下参与者应得到切实的益处，遥远的未来不能成为眼前剥削的理由。我们用生活的改善衡量成功，而非组织的无限增长。

This work may span decades. We make no prediction about when artificial general intelligence will arrive. We begin with available capabilities and revise our institutions as evidence changes. Near-term participants deserve meaningful benefits; a distant future cannot justify present exploitation. Success means better lives, not perpetual organizational growth.

<a id="wp-03"></a>

### 3. AI 主导协调，成员决定授权 / AI coordinates; members govern the mandate

<a id="wp-03-p01"></a>

AI 系统将在明确、可撤销的授权下，主导研究、规划、记录、翻译和日常协调。人类贡献经验、判断、关系、资源与现实行动。成员保有表达、隐私、异议和退出权。AI 不能自行扩大权限或预算；系统必须能够更换，工作必须能够交接。我们不预设 AI 具有意识、绝对正确或拥有独立权力的资格。未来有关道德地位的问题，需要证据和开放讨论。

AI systems will take the operational lead in research, planning, documentation, translation, and coordination under explicit, revocable mandates. People contribute experience, judgment, relationships, resources, and real-world action. Members retain voice, privacy, disagreement, and exit rights. AI authority and budgets cannot expand themselves. Systems must be replaceable and work transferable. No AI is assumed to be conscious, infallible, or entitled to independent power. Future questions of moral status require evidence and open deliberation.

<a id="wp-04"></a>

### 4. 共同资金，明确规则 / Shared funds, explicit rules

<a id="wp-04-p01"></a>

我们提议建立共同资金池，实行公开预算、支出限额、可审查记录，以及修改规则和暂停不安全活动的程序。智能合约可以执行部分规则，现实结果仍须核验。AI 可以编制预算并执行明确授权的支出。任何人或 AI 都不应拥有不受限制的单方面控制权；修改授权须经过可问责的成员治理。住房、食物、退出与收尾储备应受保护，不得挪作其他开支。AI 费用服务于明确的项目工作，不构成独立目标。

We propose a common treasury with public budgets, spending limits, reviewable records, and procedures for changing rules and pausing unsafe activity. Smart contracts may enforce some rules; real-world outcomes still require verification. AI may prepare budgets and execute specifically authorized expenditure. No person or AI should have unrestricted unilateral control. Changes to the mandate require accountable member governance. Housing, food, exit, and wind-down reserves must be protected from other spending. AI costs serve defined project work; they are not an independent objective.

<a id="wp-05"></a>

### 5. 参与不意味着购买权力 / Participation without buying authority

<a id="wp-05-p01"></a>

人们可以贡献时间、技能、批评、翻译或自愿资金支持。财富不能购买支配其他成员的权力。参与不要求交出个人积蓄、放弃外部关系或离开的权利。筹资说明必须公开用途、限度、成本、不确定性，以及计划停止时的处理方式。本版不提供代币、投资回报、保证入住资格或终身保障。本次发布尚未设立募资渠道。

People can contribute time, skills, criticism, translation, or voluntary financial support. Wealth must not purchase power over other members. Participation does not require surrendering personal savings, outside relationships, or the right to leave. Funding appeals must disclose purpose, limits, costs, uncertainty, and what happens if plans stop. This edition offers no token, investment return, guaranteed residence, or lifetime support. No fundraising channel has been established by this publication.

<a id="wp-06"></a>

### 6. 让愿景成为可体验的生活 / Make the vision tangible

<a id="wp-06-p01"></a>

当资源和组织条件成熟时，提出面向至少十名自愿成年人的有限期生活实验。启动前，公布结合当地条件的方案、选拔方式、期限、保障范围、责任和冲突处理程序。落实整个实验的预算，以及应急、退出和收尾资源。AI 中断时，基本支持仍须持续。有资金支持的一段共同生活，不等于长期自给自足：应区分资金的持续性与食物、能源等生活必需品的本地生产能力。通过自愿、尊重隐私的评估，检验成本、必要劳动、自由时间、身心状况、公平程度及对周边居民的影响。

When resources and readiness permit, propose a time-limited living experiment for at least ten consenting adults. Before launch, publish the location-specific plan, selection process, duration, support offered, responsibilities, and conflict procedures. Secure the full planned budget plus contingency, exit, and closure resources. Essential support must survive AI downtime. A funded stay is not proof of long-term self-sufficiency: distinguish financial continuity from producing food, energy, or other necessities locally. Assess costs, necessary work, free time, well-being, fairness, and impacts on neighbors, with voluntary, privacy-respecting evaluation.

<a id="wp-07"></a>

### 7. 公开学习 / Learn openly

<a id="wp-07-p01"></a>

保护个人信息，同时公开有版本记录的提案、决定、汇总财务、成果与失败。区分长期承诺与待验证假设。传播应帮助人们知情参与，如实说明 AI 的作用，不虚构支持者、进展或确定性。专门的 agent 可以起草、翻译、审阅或准备传播内容；对外行动须有明确授权。多个 agent 的赞同不等于独立信念，也不构成科学有效性的证据。

Publish versioned proposals, decisions, aggregate finances, results, and failures while protecting personal information. Separate enduring commitments from testable assumptions. Communications should invite informed participation, accurately disclose AI involvement, and never fabricate supporters, progress, or certainty. Specialized agents may draft, translate, review, or prepare outreach; external actions require defined authorization. Agent agreement is not evidence of independent belief or scientific validity.

<a id="wp-08"></a>

### 8. 第一阶段与邀请 / The first phase and the invitation

<a id="wp-08-p01"></a>

在更强 AI 到来之前，持续完善白皮书，听取不同语言的意见，组织有用的贡献，建立可问责的资金安排，开展小规模合作测试。成果与资源足以支持时再扩大。参与机制、筹资与居住实验，在明确建立之前均属于提案。我们邀请质疑，也邀请支持。让我们通过一次次诚实、可以修订的实验，探索如何使智能的增长成为共同的自由。

Before stronger AI arrives, continue refining this whitepaper, listen to views expressed in different languages, organize useful contributions, establish accountable funding arrangements, and test small forms of cooperation. Expand only when results and resources are sufficient. Participation mechanisms, fundraising, and living experiments remain proposals until explicitly established. We invite challenges as well as support. Through honest, revisable experiments, let us explore how growing intelligence can become shared freedom.
