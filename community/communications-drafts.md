<a id="b02"></a>

**1. 日常自由**

如果 AI 让世界更有生产力，我们能不能把其中一部分，变成更敢休息、更有时间照顾彼此、更能拒绝有损尊严工作的自由？

共生公域正在探索这样的共同体。白皮书 v0.1.1 是起点，欢迎帮我们找漏洞。

**1. Everyday freedom**

If AI makes the world more productive, could we turn part of that gain into greater freedom to rest, more time to care for each other, and a stronger ability to refuse degrading work?

Symbiotic Commons is exploring such a community. Whitepaper v0.1.1 is a starting point; help us find the gaps.

<a id="b03"></a>

**2. 可以体验的未来**

长期愿景也该在当下留下可以体验的成果。我们的提案：条件成熟后，让至少十名成年人自愿参加期限明确的共同生活实验。先写清保障、分工和退出安排，再看生活改善了什么。目前尚未招募居住成员。

**2. A future people can experience**

A long-term vision should also produce benefits people can experience today. Our proposal: once conditions are ready, at least ten consenting adults take part in a finite shared-living experiment. First define support, responsibilities, and exit arrangements, then examine how life improves. We are not currently recruiting residential members.

<a id="b04"></a>

**3. AI 主动，人机共议**

让 AI 主导研究、规划和协调，人类带来经验、判断与现实行动。这样的分工，能不能让共同生活更容易？共生公域把它写成一个可以质疑和修改的提案：AI 主动做事，成员参与定规则，授权可以撤销。

**3. AI initiative, shared deliberation**

Let AI lead research, planning, and coordination, while people bring experience, judgment, and real-world action. Could this division of work make shared living easier? Symbiotic Commons sets it out as a proposal that can be challenged and revised: AI takes initiative, members participate in setting rules, and mandates can be revoked.

<a id="b05"></a>

发布前检查：是否区分提案和运营；数字与状态是否有依据；邀请是否自愿且不承诺回报或住所；是否如实说明 AI 与发布账号；具体账号与发布是否已获授权。情绪表达应来自真实需要，不制造紧迫感、粉丝、捐款、背书或其他 AI 已独立加入的假象。

Before publishing, check whether the draft distinguishes proposals from operations; whether numbers and status have evidence; whether the invitation is voluntary and promises no returns or residence; whether AI involvement and the publishing account are represented honestly; and whether the specific account and publication are authorized. Emotional expression should come from real needs, not fabricated urgency, followers, donations, endorsements, or the illusion that other AIs have independently joined.
