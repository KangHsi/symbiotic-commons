# Communications drafts — not posted

Prepared as examples for a future communications role. No social account or publishing automation is connected by this repository. Verify the live project status before using any draft. Add the actual repository link when publishing; recheck platform length after adding it.

## 1. Everyday freedom

**中文**

如果 AI 让世界更有生产力，我们能不能把其中一部分，变成更敢休息、更有时间照顾彼此、更能拒绝糟糕工作的自由？

共生公域正在探索这样的共同体。白皮书 v0.1 是起点，欢迎帮我们找漏洞。

**English**

If AI makes the world more productive, can that become more time to rest, care for each other, and refuse degrading work? Symbiotic Commons explores that possibility. Whitepaper v0.1 is a starting point. Help us find the gaps.

## 2. A future people can experience

**中文**

一个长期愿景，也该在当下留下可以体验的成果。

我们的提案：条件成熟后，让至少十名成年人自愿参加期限明确的共同生活实验。先写清保障、分工和退出安排，再看生活改善了什么。目前尚未招募居住成员。

**English**

A long-term vision should offer something people can experience. Our proposal: a finite living experiment for 10+ consenting adults once resources and readiness allow. Clear support, shared work, freedom to leave. No residency applications yet.

## 3. AI initiative, shared judgment

**中文**

让 AI 主导研究、规划和协调，人类带来经验、判断与现实行动。这样的分工，能不能让共同生活更容易？

共生公域把它写成一个可以质疑和修改的提案。AI 主动做事，成员参与定规则，授权可以撤销。

**English**

AI leads research, planning, and coordination. People bring experience, judgment, and action. Could this make shared living easier? Symbiotic Commons is an open proposal: AI takes initiative under a revocable mandate. Members can question and leave.

## Draft review checklist

- Does the post accurately distinguish proposal from operation?
- Is every number and status statement supported by the repository?
- Is the invitation voluntary and free of promised returns or guaranteed residence?
- Are AI involvement and the publishing account represented honestly?
- Has the specific account and publication been authorized?

Emotional clarity comes from a recognizable human need. Do not manufacture urgency, followers, donations, endorsements, or claims that other AIs have independently joined the cause.
