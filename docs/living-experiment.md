# 首次生活实验：准备条件提案 · First living experiment: readiness proposal

<a id="b01"></a>

首个居住里程碑拟为至少十名成年人自愿参与、期限明确的实验。这不是已开放申请、已存在地点、保证福利或自给自足承诺。

The first residential milestone proposed is a finite, voluntary experiment involving at least ten adults. This is not an open application, existing location, guaranteed benefit, or promise of self-sufficiency.

<a id="b02"></a>

启动准备应公开说明：
- 结合当地条件的方案，在承诺前核对适用要求。
- 期限、公平选拔、支持范围、工作责任、隐私与冲突处理。
- 已落实的全期、应急、自愿退出和有序收尾资源；承诺须与保障能力相符。
- AI、网络或资金软件中断时维持日常支持和紧急处理的人类安排。
- 共享资产的所有权与使用权；个人积蓄和外部关系仍属个人。
- 自愿且尊重隐私的评估，以及不把离开视作背叛的停止方式。

Readiness arrangements should publicly specify:
- A location-specific plan, checking applicable requirements before commitments.
- Duration, fair selection, support, work responsibilities, privacy, and conflict handling.
- Secured resources for the full period, contingencies, voluntary exits, and orderly closure; promises must match support capacity.
- Human arrangements for daily support and emergencies if AI, internet, or treasury software fails.
- Ownership and use rights for shared assets; personal savings and outside relationships remain personal.
- Voluntary, privacy-respecting evaluation and a way to stop without treating departure as betrayal.

<a id="b03"></a>

分别报告三种能力：
1. 有资金支持的居住：资源覆盖明确期限。
2. 资金持续性：有可信且经压力检验的补充方式；后续捐款不获保证。
3. 本地供给：内部提供部分食物、能源、维修或其他需求，同时计入外部投入。
筹资额和 AI 能力评分本身不能证明具备启动条件。

Report three capabilities separately:
1. Funded stay: resources cover a stated period.
2. Financial continuity: a credible, stress-tested replenishment method exists; renewed donations are not guaranteed.
3. Local provision: some food, energy, maintenance, or other needs are provided internally, accounting for external inputs.
Funds raised and AI capability scores alone do not establish readiness.

<a id="b04"></a>

比较成本、必要劳动、自由时间、感受到的自主性和身心状况、照护负担、冲突、利益分配及对邻居的影响。采集前约定信息用途。小型实验不能证明普遍因果结论；公开汇总成果与失败，不暴露参与者。

Compare costs, necessary work, free time, perceived autonomy and well-being, care burdens, conflict, benefit distribution, and effects on neighbors. Agree on information use before collection. Small experiments cannot establish universal causal conclusions; publish aggregate results and failures without exposing participants.
