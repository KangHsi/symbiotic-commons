# A first living experiment — readiness proposal

The proposed first residential milestone is a **finite, voluntary experiment involving at least ten adults**. It is not an open application, existing location, guaranteed benefit, or promise of self-sufficiency.

## Readiness conditions to develop publicly

- A location-specific plan with applicable requirements checked before commitments.
- A defined duration, fair selection process, support offered, work responsibilities, privacy arrangements, and conflict procedure.
- Confirmed resources covering the full planned period, contingencies, voluntary exits, and orderly closure. Public promises must match secured resources.
- Human arrangements for essential daily support and emergencies if AI services, internet access, or treasury software fail.
- Clear ownership and use rights for shared assets; personal savings and outside ties remain personal.
- Voluntary, privacy-respecting evaluation and a way to stop without treating departure as betrayal.

## Three different claims

1. **Funded stay:** resources cover a stated period.
2. **Financial continuity:** a credible, stress-tested way to replenish resources exists; renewed donations are not guaranteed.
3. **Local self-provision:** some food, energy, maintenance, or other needs are provided internally, with external inputs counted.

Report these separately. Funds raised and AI benchmark scores alone cannot establish readiness.

## What to learn

Compare costs, necessary work, discretionary time, perceived autonomy and well-being, care burdens, conflict, distribution of benefits, and effects on neighbors. Agree how information will be used before collection. Small experiments cannot establish universal causal claims. Publish aggregate results and failures without exposing participants.

## 中文要点

首个居住里程碑拟为至少十名成年人自愿参与、期限明确的生活实验。地点、选拔、保障、分工、隐私、冲突处理和退出安排均须事先明确，并落实全期预算及应急、退出和收尾资源。

分别报告“有资金支持的一段生活”“资源的长期补充方式”和“生活必需品的本地供给能力”。筹资金额或 AI 能力评分不能独自证明已经具备启动条件。
