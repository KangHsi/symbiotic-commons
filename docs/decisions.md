# 编辑决策及后续修订 · Editorial decisions and subsequent revisions

<a id="b01"></a>

日期：2026-09-12。初版 v0.1 为制度提案，当前 v0.1.1 为双语编辑修订。详细依据见[决策日志](../context/DECISION_LOG.md)。

Date: 2026-09-12. The initial v0.1 is an institutional proposal; the current v0.1.1 is a bilingual editorial revision. See the [decision log](../context/DECISION_LOG.md) for detailed reasons.

<a id="b02"></a>

名称暂定为共生公域 / Symbiotic Commons，连接人机合作与共享资源，不宣称已完成商标排查。保留简洁的八节正文以方便阅读；文本不是已批准的共同体宪章。

The provisional name, 共生公域 / Symbiotic Commons, connects human–AI cooperation with shared resources; no trademark-clearance claim is made. Eight concise sections are retained for readability; the text is not an approved community constitution.

<a id="b03"></a>

初版曾发布 78 个版本、77 个语言组，独立审校未完成，也未证实 90% 人口覆盖。用户随后要求只发布中英文并严格对照；这一新决定替代广泛翻译及覆盖计划。当前只维护简体中文和英语，旧稿留在历史中。

The initial release contained 78 editions in 77 language groups, without completed independent review or verified 90% population coverage. The user subsequently requested publication only in Chinese and English with strict correspondence; this new decision supersedes broad translation and coverage plans. Only Simplified Chinese and English are now maintained; earlier drafts remain in history.

<a id="b04"></a>

AI 主动协调与可撤销授权、规则约束资金池、至少十名自愿成年人的有限期生活实验仍是制度提案。尚无资金池部署、居住运营或报名。传播稿尚未发布；复用许可仍待选择。

Active AI coordination with revocable mandates, a rule-bound treasury, and finite living experiments for at least ten consenting adults remain institutional proposals. There is no deployed treasury, residential operation, or enrollment. Communications drafts remain unpublished; a reuse license remains to be selected.

<a id="b05"></a>

先前编辑审阅与翻译使用了专门 AI agent。这不是成员投票、独立背书或未来系统认同项目价值的证据。

Earlier editorial review and translation used specialized AI agents. This is not a membership vote, independent endorsement, or evidence that future systems share the project’s values.
