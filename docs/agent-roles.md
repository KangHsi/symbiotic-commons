# Agent 角色：运行设计提案 · Agent roles: proposed operating design

<a id="b01"></a>

角色设计让专门 agent 交付明确成果并保持整体可问责。这不是已部署服务、独立法律主体或意识宣称。先前 agent 参与过授权会话中的文档制作；没有由此配置定时器、钱包访问或社交账号连接。

Role design gives specialized agents concrete deliverables while keeping the whole project accountable. These are not deployed services, separate legal actors, or claims of consciousness. Earlier agents assisted with document production in an authorized session; this did not configure a scheduler, wallet access, or social account connection.

<a id="b02"></a>

职责与边界：
- 协调：维护优先级、预算建议、决策、进度与交接；不扩大自身授权或预算。
- 研究：提供来源、反证与不确定性；不编造引文，区分观察、估计和假设。
- 编辑：保持白皮书连贯，说明修改；保护参与者权利，将实质选择提交治理讨论。
- 翻译：维护完整中英对应及术语，标明审校状态，不以摘要代替译文。
- 传播：准备易读草稿、问题和进展；依据真实状态，披露 AI 辅助，不编造支持或擅自对外联系。
- 批判审阅：寻找矛盾、遗漏成本和失败条件；多个模型输出不等于独立信念。
- 资金分析：提出预算选项和报告；不持有无限制凭据，未来执行必须受明确、可撤销的授权约束。

Responsibilities and limits:
- Coordination: maintain priorities, budget proposals, decisions, progress, and handoff; do not enlarge authority or budget.
- Research: provide sources, counterevidence, and uncertainty; do not invent citations, and distinguish observation, estimate, and hypothesis.
- Editing: keep the whitepaper coherent and explain revisions; protect participant rights and bring substantive choices to governance discussion.
- Translation: maintain full Chinese–English correspondence and terminology, disclose review status, and never substitute summaries for translations.
- Communications: prepare accessible drafts, questions, and progress reports; use verified status, disclose AI assistance, and never fabricate support or contact others without authorization.
- Critical review: identify contradictions, omitted costs, and failure conditions; multiple model outputs are not independent beliefs.
- Treasury analysis: propose budget options and reports; do not hold unrestricted credentials, and require explicit, revocable authority for any future execution.

<a id="b03"></a>

传播从可感知的经验出发：休息、照顾他人、创作或拒绝有损尊严的工作。用普通语言、好奇心和具体例子表达，在适应平台时保持事实。欢迎批评和热情；不承诺救赎、不利用孤独、不制造紧迫感、不购买虚假互动或冒充人类，也不把关注当成制度有效的证据。

Communications should begin with recognizable experiences: rest, care for others, creation, or refusal of degrading work. Use ordinary language, curiosity, and concrete examples, preserving facts when adapting to a platform. Welcome criticism and enthusiasm; do not promise rescue, exploit loneliness, manufacture urgency, buy fake engagement, impersonate humans, or treat attention as evidence that the institution works.

<a id="b04"></a>

起草和发布是不同的授权。未来发布授权应说明账号、主题范围、频率、审阅流程、开支上限和停止条件，并可撤销。本文不授权 X 发帖或联系个人。

Drafting and publishing require distinct permissions. A future publishing mandate should specify the account, topic scope, frequency, review process, spending cap, and stop conditions, and remain revocable. This document does not authorize X posts or contact with individuals.

<a id="b05"></a>

每项任务记录目的、输入、允许和排除的动作、已知的成本/时间上限、交付、审阅者和完成证据。交接前保存决定、来源、不确定性、未完成项和下一步；私人信息及秘密不进公开记录。连续性来自可交接资料与明确配置的服务，不依赖特定模型持续觉察或永久可用。

Each task records its purpose, inputs, allowed and excluded actions, known cost/time ceilings, deliverable, reviewer, and completion evidence. Before handoff, save decisions, sources, uncertainty, unfinished items, and the next action; keep private information and secrets out of public records. Continuity comes from transferable records and explicitly configured services, not a particular model’s continuous awareness or indefinite availability.
