# 下一版待讨论问题 · Open questions for the next edition

<a id="b01"></a>

这些问题尚未有最终答案。欢迎针对具体章节提出理由、反例和修改建议；不能把未解决的价值选择写成共识。

These questions have no final answers yet. Reasons, counterexamples, and suggested changes to specific sections are welcome; unresolved value choices must not be presented as consensus.

<a id="b02"></a>

1. 最先保障什么？时间、食物、住房、照护或其他需求；成员福利与更广泛互助的公平边界在哪里？
2. 谁是成员？如何界定加入、投票、责任、利益冲突、申诉与退出，避免财富变成支配权？
3. 如何修改规则？哪些保护需要更强共识，人们如何挑战人类决定和 AI 建议？
4. 如何设计资金池？部署前比较协议执行、保管、隐私、现实核验、恢复和实际法律安排。
5. 有限捐款如何形成持久支持？评估真实资源流，不预设代币升值、持续捐赠或特定商业模式。

1. What should be guaranteed first? Time, food, housing, care, or another need; where is the fair boundary between member benefits and wider mutual support?
2. Who is a member? How should admission, voting, responsibilities, conflicts of interest, appeals, and exit be defined without turning wealth into power over others?
3. How are rules changed? Which protections need stronger agreement, and how can people challenge human decisions and AI recommendations?
4. How should the treasury be designed? Compare protocol execution, custody, privacy, real-world verification, recovery, and practical legal arrangements before deployment.
5. How can limited donations become durable support? Evaluate real resource flows without assuming token appreciation, perpetual giving, or a particular business model.

<a id="b03"></a>

6. 什么让首个生活实验值得开展？明确可行期限、保障、预算、公平选拔和停止条件。
7. 什么算失败？例如把负担转给无偿照护者、依赖单一资助者、决策不透明、成本增长却无收益、压制异议。
8. 如何保持中英文完整对应？确定术语、逐段审阅和同步流程；当前不扩展其他语言或追求人口覆盖指标。
9. AI 能力变化如何影响授权？依据实际可靠性与生活结果，而非技术标签；道德地位与当前工具权限分开讨论。
10. 采用何种复用许可？明确文档许可和贡献政策后，再称为正式开放许可。

6. What makes the first living experiment worthwhile? Define a feasible duration, support, budget, fair selection, and stop conditions.
7. What counts as failure? Examples include shifting burdens to unpaid carers, dependence on one benefactor, opaque decisions, rising costs without benefit, and suppressed dissent.
8. How do we maintain full Chinese–English correspondence? Define terminology, paragraph review, and synchronization; current work does not expand into other languages or pursue a population-coverage metric.
9. How should changes in AI capabilities affect mandates? Use observed reliability and life outcomes, not technical labels; discuss moral status separately from current tool authority.
10. Which reuse license should apply? Establish a document license and contribution policy before describing the project as formally open-licensed.
