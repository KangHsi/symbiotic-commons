# Open questions for the next edition / 下一版待讨论问题

1. **What should be guaranteed first?** Time, food, housing support, care, or another need—and what is a fair boundary between member benefits and wider solidarity?
2. **Who is a member?** How are admission, voting, responsibilities, conflicts of interest, appeals, and exit defined without turning wealth into authority?
3. **How are the rules changed?** Which protections should require stronger agreement, and how can people challenge both a human decision and an AI recommendation?
4. **How should a treasury be structured?** Compare protocol enforcement, custody, privacy, real-world verification, recovery, and practical legal arrangements before deployment.
5. **How do limited donations become durable support?** Evaluate realistic resource flows without assuming token appreciation, perpetual giving, or a particular business model.
6. **What makes the first living experiment worthwhile?** Define a feasible duration, support package, budget, fair selection, and stop conditions.
7. **What would count as failure?** Examples include shifting burdens to unpaid carers, dependence on one benefactor, opaque decisions, escalating costs without benefit, and suppressed dissent.
8. **How do we measure language access?** Obtain overlap-aware population evidence and fluent translation review; address nonreaders, children, disability, and offline access.
9. **How should future AI capabilities change the mandate?** Use observed reliability and social outcomes, not a label alone. Keep questions of moral status separate from present-day tool authority.
10. **What reuse license fits the project?** Select a clear document license and contribution policy before describing the repository as formally open-licensed.

这些问题尚未有最终答案。欢迎围绕具体章节提出理由、反例与修改建议。初版不将未解决的价值选择伪装为已经达成的共识。
