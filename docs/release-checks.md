# v0.1 preparation checks

Date: 2026-09-12.

## What was checked

- 78 complete whitepaper editions across 77 editorial language groups, with Simplified and Traditional Chinese counted as one group.
- Eight ordered sections in every edition, UTF-8 readability, version references, local navigation links, and file hashes recorded in the translation manifest.
- Cross-agent semantic spot-review of English, Simplified Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Arabic, Persian, Hindi, and Indonesian. No material discrepancy remained in that reviewed sample after corrections. This is AI review, not independent fluent-speaker certification.
- Corrected Indonesian and Javanese wording for “necessary work” to avoid suggesting compulsory labor.
- Editorial review of the project introduction, contributor guidance, role definitions, experiment conditions, decisions, and coverage methodology for consistency and proposal-versus-operation status.
- An independent recalculation of the exploratory CLDR lower bound described in the coverage note reproduced approximately 74.16% with all listed languages. This is a method limitation, not the reach of this release.
- Checked intended publication files for obvious local private paths, credential markers, and accidental intermediate files. This is not a formal security audit.

## What remains unverified

- Independent fluent-speaker accuracy and culturally appropriate wording for every edition. All translations retain draft status; some have priority-review notes.
- Deduplicated worldwide population reach of at least 90%. No achieved percentage is claimed.
- Fulah and Wolof: not published or counted; fluent translators are needed.
- Real-world funding, treasury, membership, residence, or continuous agent operation. This repository describes proposals and does not establish these services.

A structural check cannot substitute for language review or practical evidence that a social institution works.
