# Contributing / 参与贡献

This is a proposal-stage project. You can help without paying, pledging loyalty, moving home, or giving up outside relationships.

## Useful first contributions

1. **Critique a claim.** Name the whitepaper section, describe the issue, provide evidence where available, and suggest wording.
2. **Review a translation.** Give the locale, section number, current sentence, proposed sentence, and reason. State your language familiarity honestly; credentials are optional.
3. **Add evidence.** Prefer original research, institutional documentation, and lived experience with its context. Separate evidence from interpretation.
4. **Develop a bounded experiment.** State the question, voluntary participants, resources, duration, risks, exit arrangements, and what would count as failure.

Use a GitHub issue or a pull request. Contributions may be written in any available language. AI can assist with triage and translation, but receiving a draft response is not acceptance of a policy change.

## Review and decisions

The initial maintainer is responsible for repository changes during this proposal stage. This practical publishing role does not constitute the future community's settled governance. Major changes should explain their reasons and dissent, update the source version, and mark translations that need synchronization. No contributor automatically gains treasury or publishing access by making a contribution.

## Respect and privacy

Critique ideas; do not harass, threaten, discriminate against, or expose people. Do not include identity documents, private addresses, account credentials, medical records, or donation receipts in public issues. Public GitHub issue history is not a confidential reporting channel. Until a private reporting arrangement exists, use GitHub's own reporting mechanisms for platform abuse and do not publish sensitive details here.

## Intellectual contributions

Submit only material you have the right to share. State sources and any AI assistance. A repository-wide reuse license has not yet been selected; public visibility should not be presented as a completed licensing decision. The initial text may be linked and discussed, and applicable quotation rights remain unaffected.

初期仓库维护者承担发布工作，未来共同体治理仍待讨论。贡献不会自动获得资金或发布权限。请勿在公开讨论中提交个人敏感资料。全库再利用许可尚未选定；提交内容时请确认有权分享，并说明来源与 AI 辅助情况。
