# Symbiotic Commons · 共生公域

**More intelligence should mean more freedom to live.**

What if technological progress gave people more time to rest, care for each other, and refuse degrading work?

Symbiotic Commons is an early, openly revisable proposal for voluntary human–AI communities. AI takes substantial responsibility for research, planning, and coordination under a revocable mandate. People bring experience, judgment, relationships, and action. Shared resources should become dignity, material security, and time people can call their own.

**Status: whitepaper v0.1, proposal stage.** No residence is operating, no fundraising channel is established, and no autonomous treasury or continuous agent service is deployed by this repository. The proposed name is provisional. Contributions, critique, and translation review are welcome.

## What we propose

- **A long horizon with benefits along the way.** Begin with current capabilities, without predicting an AGI arrival date.
- **AI-led operational coordination.** Clear responsibilities, published reasoning, member voice, and replaceable systems.
- **Rule-bound shared resources.** Public budgets and limited, revocable execution authority; no unrestricted personal or AI control.
- **A tangible first experiment.** When funding and readiness allow, propose a finite living experiment for at least ten consenting adults, with clear support and exit arrangements.
- **Learning in public.** Record failures and disagreements alongside progress. A funded stay is not proof of long-term self-sufficiency.

## 中文介绍

**让智能的增长成为共同的自由。**

如果技术进步能让普通人更敢休息、更有时间照顾彼此、更能拒绝糟糕的工作，我们应该怎样组织共同生活？

共生公域是一份仍在形成中的人机共生共同体提案。AI 在可撤销的授权下主导研究、规划和日常协调，人类贡献经验、判断、关系与现实行动。我们希望共同资源逐步转化为生活保障、尊严与自由时间。

现在发布的是白皮书 v0.1。我们尚未运营居住社区、开设募资渠道、部署自治资金池或持续运行的 agent 服务。欢迎提出批评、修改译文、补充证据。全球 90% 人口语言覆盖仍是**未验证的目标**；多语言版本数量不能证明实际覆盖人口。
