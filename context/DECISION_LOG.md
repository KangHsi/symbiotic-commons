## 不要混淆的三组概念

1. **筹得某次实验经费 / 持续获得补充资金 / 本地生产自给：** 三者是不同程度的能力，需要各自的证据。
2. **AI 主动负责工作 / AI 独占权力：** 前者是当前协作要求；后者不是本项目的已采纳制度。
3. **长期愿景 / 已部署组织：** 白皮书、角色说明和传播草稿不能证明资金、成员、居住服务或自主系统已经存在。

## English decision digest

The user's later clarification superseded a mandatory “profitable startup first” pathway: vision and institutional development should lead. AI leadership means active intellectual and operational contribution, not human submission. Continuity should be transferable across people and systems. Proposed treasury controls bind both humans and AI; no system may expand its own mandate or budget. Tangible finite living experiments for at least ten consenting adults should connect the long horizon to present benefits. These institutions remain proposals. The 90% language-access target remains unresolved despite 78 published editions. Public documentation and local handoff are authorized; social posting, financial execution and continuous operation are not established by these records.
